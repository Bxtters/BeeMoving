This file names the two models in this folder. It is not loaded by the game.
movenet_lightning.tflite is one person. The camera stage uses it when Settings asks for one person.
movenet_multipose.tflite is up to six people. The camera stage uses it when Settings asks for more than one person.

MoveNet SinglePose Lightning, float16
Source: https://tfhub.dev/google/lite-model/movenet/singlepose/lightning/tflite/float16/4
License: Apache License 2.0

Input:  uint8 NHWC [1, 192, 192, 3], RGB, values 0-255
Output: float32 [1, 1, 17, 3]
        last dimension is (y, x, score), y and x normalized 0-1, origin top-left

Keypoints:
  0 nose
  1 left eye      2 right eye
  3 left ear      4 right ear
  5 left shoulder 6 right shoulder
  7 left elbow    8 right elbow
  9 left wrist    10 right wrist
  11 left hip     12 right hip
  13 left knee    14 right knee
  15 left ankle   16 right ankle
